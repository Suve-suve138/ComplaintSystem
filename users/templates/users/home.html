How It Works

Simple 4-step process from complaint to resolution

Register

Create your account using Aadhaar-verified details or mobile number.

2

Select the department, describe your issue, and attach supporting documents.

Key Features

Built for transparency and citizen empowerment

Role-Based Access

Citizens, department officers, and administrators each have tailored dashboards with appropriate permissions.

Real-Time Tracking

Live status updates via SMS and email at every stage — submission, assignment, under review, and resolution.

Analytics Dashboard

Department-wise performance reports, resolution timelines, and compliance metrics for full accountability.

Secure & Private

End-to-end encrypted submissions. Your data is protected and never shared without consent.

Multi-Language Support

File complaints in Hindi, English, or your regional language — all 22 scheduled languages supported.

SLA Compliance

Automated escalation when complaints breach service-level agreements, ensuring timely resolution.
